# Tamil phrase of the day

A practical, colloquial Chennai Tamil phrase every morning at 8 (IST). Code: [server/modules/tamil.mjs](../server/modules/tamil.mjs), page: `/tamil`.

- Tamil script, easy transliteration, meaning, when to use it, and a likely reply.
- A theme per week (autos and directions, food, money, office small talk...), no repeats.
- Sunday quiz on the week's phrases.
